# Research inbox

New findings, books and ideas go here first. Nothing goes into the manual until it passes the intake test.

## Intake test

A finding earns a place in the manual only if all three are true:

1. **It is strong evidence.** A meta-analysis, a preregistered replication, a large trial or an expert consensus statement. A single small study, a podcast claim or a viral thread does not qualify on its own.
2. **It would change an action.** It alters a standard, a protocol or an evidence rating. Interesting but inert findings stay here.
3. **It survives the opposite search.** You looked for the best evidence against it and it still holds.

## How to add an entry

Copy the template, fill it in, and leave the decision blank until the next sweep.

```

```
