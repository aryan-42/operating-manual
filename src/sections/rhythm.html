13Rhythm

The small set of recurring actions that keeps all of this alive. Miss everything else before you miss the weekly review.

Daily · three non-negotiables

Choose three actions so small you can do them on your worst day. They are the Floor of your Floor.

Workbook · daily

Example: move for 30 minutes. One hour of deep work. In bed by a fixed time.
The 15-minute version for travel, illness or chaos. Example: 20 push-ups, 5 pages, one message to someone in the inner five.

Weekly · 20 minutes, same slot

  1. What did I do this week that the man described in Direction would do?
  2. Where did I miss, and what in the environment caused it?
  3. What is the one adjustment for next week?
  4. Training sessions, deep work blocks and inner-five time: scheduled for next week?
  5. Wins log and money: updated?

Workbook · weekly

One dated line per week. Trim old lines each quarter.

Monthly · 30 minutes

  • Body measurements and best lifts into Baseline tests.
  • Savings rate into Baseline tests and Money.
  • One feedback conversation at work.
  • Tick any standards you have newly met.

Quarterly · 90 minutes

  • Rescore in the Scorecard and save a snapshot.
  • Pick one focus domain for the next 90 days, usually the lowest score.
  • Archive the finished sprint and set up the next one on the Sprint board.
  • Reread your code and your anti-vision.

Annual · every 14 November

Half a day, alone, away from your desk. Read the whole manual. Rewrite Direction if you have changed. Answer the four questions below and keep every year's answers.

Workbook · annual reviews

For each year: what I am proud of, what I avoided, what I learned about myself, what I am changing.

Backup

Your entries save to your Claude account when you open this page signed in, and to this browser otherwise. Once a year, copy a backup and paste it into a note you control.
