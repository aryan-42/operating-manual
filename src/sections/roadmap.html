12Roadmap

Ten years in five phases. Each phase has one job and a test for leaving it. Dates assume you start in November 2026 at 24.

FoundationNov 2026 to joining day
age 24

Job: install the base habits while your schedule is still your own.

  • Body Floor complete: 12 weeks of lifting, sleep fixed, baseline numbers recorded.
  • Direction written. First scorecard snapshot saved.
  • Weekly review running. Daily reading running.
  • Finish the MBA cleanly and arrive at work with one automation project already scoped.

Exit when: Twelve weeks of training logged, Body Floor mostly ticked, sleep steady, Direction written, three snapshots saved, one portfolio piece published.

ProveFirst 12 months at work
age 24 to 25

Job: become undeniably good at the role and automate your finances.

  • Craft Floor complete. First measurable win logged within 90 days.
  • Money Floor complete: emergency fund, health cover, 20% invested automatically.
  • Training survives the move to full-time work. This is where most people lose it.
  • Presence Floor complete. Speaking log started.

Exit when: Craft and Money Floors ticked, three wins logged with numbers, six months of expenses in cash, training unbroken through the move, a good first appraisal.

Differentiate2028 to 2029
age 26 to 27

Job: build the spike. Be known for one thing.

  • Craft at Strong. Public portfolio live. First step toward a revenue-adjacent role, inside or outside.
  • Body at Strong. A physical skill started.
  • Savings rate at 30 to 40%. Second income source earns its first rupee.
  • Mentors and a sponsor in place. Inner five maintained through any city change.

Exit when: Craft and Body at Strong, a public portfolio of six pieces, known for one thing inside the company, savings rate at 30% or more, a sponsor in place.

Leverage2030 to 2032
age 28 to 30

Job: turn skill into ownership.

  • Lead a team, a product or a P&L. Or found the company if the side project has paying users.
  • All seven domains at Strong. Two at Elite.
  • Partner and family decisions made on purpose.
  • Start mentoring others.

Exit when: You own a team, product or P&L, or the company has passed gate 4. All seven domains at Strong and two at Elite.

Compound2033 to 2036
age 31 to 34

Job: hold the standard and widen the mission.

  • Senior leadership or a company of your own. Income from three sources.
  • 5 to 10 years of expenses invested. Parents' plan funded.
  • Elite Body numbers held, not just touched once.
  • The mission in Direction is visible in how you spend your weeks.

Exit when: There is no exit. The measure is that the standards hold with less effort each year and that other people are better off for it.

Reading this again in a year or three

Find your phase by age. Then check the exit test of the phase before it. If that test is not met, you are still in the earlier phase, whatever your age. Do not compress two phases into one. The order matters more than the dates. The first eighteen months are laid out month by month in Year one.
