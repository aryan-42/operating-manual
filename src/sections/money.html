00Money

Wayne inherited his. You build yours with a savings rate, time and rising income.

General information for an Indian salaried professional. This is not personalised financial advice. Tax rules, limits and products change, so check current rules each April and use a SEBI-registered fee-only adviser for real decisions. Tax sections are not quoted here because India's income-tax law was renumbered in 2026.

One-page summary

The standard
Spend less than you earn by a wide margin, invest the difference automatically in low-cost funds, carry no consumer debt, and grow income faster than lifestyle.
Three actions
1. Automate investing on salary day. 2. Build six months of expenses in cash. 3. Send half of every raise to investments.
Watch one number
Savings rate: money saved or invested, divided by take-home pay.
Before the first salary
Track a month of spending. Open the accounts. Decide the split below so that day one is automatic.

Standards

Floor first 12 months of salary

Strong year 2 to 4

Elite year 5 to 10

The "years of expenses" targets are arithmetic from a 40 to 50% savings rate held from age 24, before investment returns. They are checkpoints, not promises.

What the evidence says

  • Most active funds lose to the index. S&P's scorecard for India reports that a firm majority of actively managed funds in every category underperformed their benchmark over the decade to December 2025, including about 83% of tax-saver equity funds. Critics note the benchmarks are not all directly investable, so read it as direction, not a precise figure.32
  • Trading derivatives loses money for almost everyone. SEBI's own study found 93% of more than one crore individual futures and options traders lost money between FY22 and FY24, about ₹2 lakh each on average.33
  • Money and happiness. For most people happiness keeps rising with income. It stops rising only for the least happy group.20 Beyond basic security, relationships move wellbeing far more than wealth does.19
  • Negotiating is worth it. New hires who negotiated gained about $5,000 in starting pay on average in one US survey.34 Early pay differences compound through every later raise.

Protocol by stage

Stage 0Before the first salary
  • Track every rupee for one month.
  • Open a salary account, a separate savings account for the emergency fund, and a direct mutual fund account. Complete KYC.
  • Read the offer letter line by line: fixed pay, variable pay, provident fund, gratuity, notice period, any bond.
  • Estimate monthly take-home and write the split below.
Stage 1Months 1 to 12
  • Automate the split on salary day. Never rely on what is left at month end.
  • Fill the emergency fund first. Start a small index-fund instalment alongside it so the habit exists.
  • Buy personal health cover. Choose the tax regime with a calculator, not by habit.
  • Get one credit card, set it to pay the full bill automatically, and use it for one fixed expense.
Stage 2Year 2 to 4
  • Emergency fund is full, so its share moves to investing. Savings rate reaches 30 to 40%.
  • Half of every raise goes to investments before it reaches spending.
  • Start the second income source from your Craft: paid writing, consulting, a small tool.
  • Term life cover when someone depends on your income.
Stage 3Year 5 onward
  • Review allocation once a year and rebalance. Ignore the market in between.
  • Fund your parents' plan. Write a will.
  • Large decisions through the frameworks in Decisions: house, car, starting up.

The 50, 30, 20 split is a common starting heuristic and is judgement. Your real numbers replace it after three months of tracking.

Emergency fund and insurance the defence comes before the offence
  • Emergency fund. Six months of total monthly spend. Keep it boring and reachable within a day: a separate savings account, sweep-in deposit or liquid fund. It is not an investment and does not need a return.
  • What it is for. Job loss, medical costs not covered, an urgent trip home, a broken laptop you need for work. Not holidays or phones.
  • Health insurance. A personal policy in addition to the employer's group cover, because the group cover ends when the job does. Buying young keeps premiums low and waiting periods behind you. Check your parents' cover too. Their medical bills are the largest risk to your plan.
  • Term life insurance. Buy it when someone depends on your income or you take a large loan. Plain term cover only.
  • Keep insurance and investment separate. Products that mix the two, such as endowment and unit-linked plans, usually do both jobs poorly.

Cover amounts depend on your city, family and health. Ask an adviser who charges a fee and earns no commission.

A simple default. One or two broad index funds bought every month on salary day, plus the provident fund you already have. Decide a split between stocks and safe assets that you could hold through a fall of 40% without selling. Review once a year. That is the whole strategy.

Tax basics what to understand, without the section numbers
  • India has two tax regimes: one with lower rates and few deductions, one with higher rates and more deductions. Run your own numbers through the government calculator each year and pick the cheaper one.
  • Your employer deducts tax every month based on what you declare. Declare accurately in April and again when asked for proofs.
  • Read your salary slip until you can explain every line: basic, allowances, provident fund, professional tax, tax deducted.
  • File your own return on the government portal at least once. Match it against the annual tax statement and your Form 16.
  • Capital gains on funds and shares are taxed when you sell, at rates that depend on the asset and holding period. Buying and holding defers this.
  • Never choose an investment only for a tax saving. A bad product with a deduction is still a bad product.
Credit and debt use credit, never carry it
  • A credit score runs from 300 to 900. Above 750 is generally treated as good by lenders.
  • It is built by paying on time, using a small part of your limit, and keeping old accounts open.
  • Pay the full statement amount, automatically. The minimum-due option is the most expensive loan you can take.
  • No "buy now, pay later", personal loans or instalment plans for things that lose value.
  • An education loan or a sensible home loan can be reasonable debt. Clear the costliest loan first.
  • Do not lend, or sign as guarantor, an amount you cannot afford to lose. If you lend to family or friends, treat it as a gift in your own accounts.
Big purchases phone, vehicle, home
  • The 30-day rule. For anything over an amount you set, wait 30 days. Most wants do not survive it.
  • Vehicle. A common heuristic: at least 20% down, a loan of four years or less, and total running cost under a tenth of income. Prefer no loan at all in your twenties.
  • Home. Renting is not waste when you may change city. Compare the full cost of owning (interest, maintenance, taxes, the return your down payment would have earned) against rent. Buy when you expect to stay seven years or more, and the instalment fits inside your needs bucket.
  • Status goods. Watches, phones and cars are signals. Buy them from the wants bucket after the future bucket is paid, or not at all.

These are heuristics and judgement. Use the house, vehicle and job frameworks in Decisions for the actual choice.

The second income a ladder from your Craft
  1. Sell time at a premium. Paid workshops, freelance analysis or automation work in your niche.
  2. Sell a result. Fixed-price projects: you are paid for the outcome, not the hours.
  3. Sell a product. A template, a small tool, a course. Built once, sold many times.
  4. Own equity. Shares in something you help build.

Each rung needs the one below it. Check your employment contract before taking outside paid work, and declare the income.

Scams and expensive mistakes how money is usually lost
  • Any promise of guaranteed high returns. Guaranteed and high do not occur together.
  • Futures, options, intraday trading and tips from groups or influencers. Nine in ten individuals lose.
  • Insurance sold as investment by a relative, a bank relationship manager or an agent.
  • Crypto or stock "courses" where the product being sold is the course.
  • Calls, links or messages asking for an OTP, a PIN or remote access. No bank or official asks for these.
  • Lifestyle rising to match every raise.
  • Selling investments during a market fall.
Parents the plan most people leave too late
  • Know their health cover, its limit and when it renews.
  • Know where their documents, accounts and nominations are.
  • Agree, as a family, what support looks like: a monthly amount, a medical reserve, or both.
  • Add a parents' medical line to your emergency planning. It is the single biggest shock to plans like this one.

How this domain fails, and what to ignore

  • Buying the watch and the car to look like the destination before reaching it.
  • Optimising small expenses while ignoring income growth. The Craft section is your real money plan.
  • Waiting to invest until you "know enough". The automatic index instalment needs no knowledge.
  • Checking the portfolio daily.
  • Ignore: stock tips, trading screenshots, "passive income" schemes that need recruiting, and anyone whose income depends on you buying the product.
