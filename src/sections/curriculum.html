How to study a subject for a year

  1. One standard text, worked through with the exercises. A course is fine if it has graded problems.
  2. One project that forces you to use it on something real.
  3. Recall notes at the end of each chapter, reviewed on the spacing in Mind.
  4. One piece published each quarter explaining something you learned.
  5. A test at the end: can you teach the core of it to a colleague in thirty minutes without notes?
