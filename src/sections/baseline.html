00Baseline tests

Measured starting points. Fill the first column once and never change it. Update the second column every month or quarter.

A 1 to 10 self-rating drifts with mood. These numbers do not. The gap between the two columns, a year from now, is the most honest progress report you will have.

Date of the baseline, then the date of each retest.

If a number is embarrassing, good. It is the number you will be proudest to have recorded in two years.
