00Assets and constraints

A plan that ignores your real starting position is a wish. State both sides plainly, once, and update them yearly.

What you start with

  • An uncommon combination. An engineering degree, an MBA in HR and the ability to build software and AI automation. Few people hold all three.
  • A job in hand. You begin your career with an offer accepted, which removes the pressure most of your cohort is under and frees the coming months for foundation work.
  • Time. At 24, ten years of compounding still leaves you at 34.
  • Proof that you ship. You already build and finish projects. That habit transfers.

How to think about constraints

A constraint is a fact that narrows your options: a credential, a location, money, an obligation, your health. It is different from an excuse, which is a constraint you have not tested. For each one, write the workaround. Most early-career constraints stop mattering once you have two or three results with numbers attached.
