Where year one usually breaks

  • Work month 1. A new city and long days end the training habit. Decide where and when you will train before you arrive.
  • Work months 2 to 4. First salary meets first freedom. Automate the split before the first pay lands.
  • Work months 5 to 8. Old friendships fade through distance. Put the calls in the calendar.
  • Work months 9 to 12. Comparison with classmates' titles and pay. Compare with your own snapshot from a year earlier.
