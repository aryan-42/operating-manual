00Myth file

Popular self-improvement claims that did not survive testing. Most "high performance" content is built on at least one of these.

A man chasing the top 1% is the target customer for every one of these ideas. Knowing which ones failed saves years. Each row gives the claim, what happened when it was tested properly, and what to do with the time instead.

ClaimVerdictWhat the evidence showsDo this instead
Power poses raise testosterone and make you bolderFailedA replication with 200 people found no effect on hormones or on any of three behavioural tasks. People only reported feeling more powerful. A lead author of the original study no longer believes the effect.29Prepare. The prep file in Craft changes outcomes. Posture is for your back.
Willpower is a tank that runs dryFailedEffect near zero across 36 labs.12Design the environment so the decision never comes up.
10,000 hours makes an expertOverstatedPractice explains 18 to 26% of skill in sports, music and games and under 1% in professions.13In a career, choose harder problems and better feedback over more hours.
Study in your learning style: visual, auditory, kinaestheticFailedA commissioned review found virtually no properly designed study supporting it, and several that contradicted it.30Test yourself and space your reviews. That works for everyone.14
A growth mindset transforms achievementContestedAverage effect of programmes on achievement is close to zero, with possible benefit for at-risk students.15Build systems that make effort automatic. Belief follows results.
Grit is the secret of successRepackagedNearly identical to conscientiousness and only moderately tied to performance.18Practise plain reliability: do what you said, on time, checked.
It takes 21 days to form a habitWrongMedian 66 days, range 18 to 254.8Budget two to eight months per habit.
You need heavy barbells to build muscleHalf trueAcross 21 studies, light and heavy loads built similar muscle when sets were taken to failure. Heavy loads were better for maximal strength.22Train hard with what you own now. Add heavy loading when you have access.
A drink a day is good for youNot at your ageFor men aged 15 to 39 the intake that minimises health risk is about a tenth of a standard drink a day, which is effectively none.26Treat alcohol as an occasional cost, never a health habit.
More money will not make you happier past a thresholdOutdatedHappiness keeps rising with income for most people. It plateaus only for the least happy group.20Earn more, and fix unhappiness at its real source.
Let the anger out: hit a bag, go for a hard runFailedAcross 154 studies and 10,189 people, activities that raise arousal did not reduce anger. Activities that calm the body did.45Slow the breath, wait, then decide. The steps are in Character.
Multitasking gets more doneFailedIn controlled experiments, switching between tasks cost time on every switch, and more as the tasks became more complex.59Single-task blocks. They are a core part of Mind.
93% of communication is body language and toneMisreadThe figures come from two small 1960s experiments on mixed messages about feelings. The researcher himself says they do not apply outside that situation.58Prepare what you will say. Words carry the content.
Alpha males lead, and status is won by dominanceWrong modelThe idea came from captive wolves. The researcher who popularised it later showed that wild packs are families, and the "alpha" pair are simply the parents.57 Applying it to people is a further leap with no support here.Earn standing through competence and generosity. That part is judgement.
An expert can predict which couples will lastOverstatedThe famous accuracy figures came from equations fitted to the same couples they described, and such equations lose much of their accuracy on new data.40 In 43 datasets, change in relationship quality over time was largely unpredictable.39Judge how the two of you treat each other now, over a full year.
Find the right type of person and the relationship will workOverstatedAcross 11,196 couples, the traits of either partner added little once you knew how each person experienced the relationship itself.39Use the five things to watch in People.
A dopamine detox resets your brainMisnamedA Harvard Health article by a physician explains that avoiding stimulation does not lower dopamine, that the idea's own author says the name is not literal, and that the extreme versions deprive people of healthy things for no reason. This is expert commentary, not a trial.60Cut the one compulsive input that is costing you. Keep food, exercise, music and people.
"Testosterone-boosting" supplements workFailedA review of 50 products found that 90% claimed to raise testosterone, under a quarter had any supporting data, and a tenth contained ingredients with data showing a decrease.61Sleep, training, body fat and alcohol are what move it. See a doctor if you suspect a real deficiency.
Winners wake at 5 a.m.UnsupportedNothing in the research used here favours a particular hour. What is supported is getting 7 hours or more5 and keeping sleep and wake times regular.23Fix a wake time you can hold seven days a week. Never cut sleep to reach it.

The filter for anything new

Before adopting any technique, ask three questions. Was it tested on more than a few dozen people? Did anyone outside the original team repeat it? Would it change what I do this week? If you cannot answer yes to all three, park it in the research inbox in the repository.
