Reading the hit rate

  • Above 85%. The targets are right. Raise them slightly next sprint.
  • 60 to 85%. Normal. Keep the targets and fix the one cue or slot that keeps failing.
  • Below 60%. The plan is too big for your current life. Halve it. A small plan kept beats a large one abandoned.
