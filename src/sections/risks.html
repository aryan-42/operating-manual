00Risk register

The ten things most likely to knock a decade off course. Each has an early sign, a defence to put in place now, and a first response.

Reread this at every annual review. A risk you have named and prepared for is an inconvenience. The same event unprepared can cost years.

Tripwires

A tripwire is a rule decided in a calm hour that forces a decision in a bad one. "If my savings rate is under 20% for two quarters, I cancel every subscription and reset the split." "If I miss training for ten days, I do the minimum session that evening." Write one for each of your three largest risks.
