00Time audit

Every week has 168 hours. Find out where yours go before deciding where they should.

Method

  1. For 7 consecutive days, note what you did in each waking hour. A note on your phone at each meal and before bed is enough.
  2. Add the hours into the categories below.
  3. Set a target for each category, using the Ideal week as a reference.
  4. Repeat once a year, and after any change of city or job.
