The manual holds everything. Your week runs on very little of it. This page shows which parts to use and when.

The first two weeks

  1. Day 1, 45 minutes. Do the Baseline tests you can do today and score yourself in the Scorecard. Save the snapshot.
  2. Day 2 to 7. Run the time audit and the 7-night sleep log from Baseline tests. Start Body, Stage 1, Week 1. It needs only the kit you already own.
  3. End of week 1, 60 minutes. Fill in Direction. This decides what the next ten years are for.
  4. Week 2. Set up your first 12-week sprint on the Sprint board, with Body as the focus. Put the weekly review in your calendar.
  5. Every quarter. Retest, rescore, archive the sprint, choose the next focus.

Three depths

  • Summary. Each domain opens with a one-page summary. That is enough to run a quarter.
  • Protocol. The staged plan underneath tells you what to do each week.
  • Playbooks. The collapsed rows marked with a plus are step-by-step guides for specific situations. Open one when you need it. "Open all playbooks" at the top of the page expands every one.

Two rules that protect the system

One focus per quarter. Every other domain runs at its Floor. Overhauling seven areas at once is the most reliable way to quit by week three.

Reading is not doing. Adding content to this manual, rebuilding it as an app, or studying a domain you are not working on all feel like progress. Only ticked standards and retested numbers count.

Everything you type or tick is saved automatically, and each standard records the date you first met it. Use the backup at the bottom of Rhythm once a year. Body, Mind, Craft, Money and Presence are complete in this version. People and Character, and two new domains, get the same depth in the next release.
