Protocol

  • Write the code. Ten lines you will not cross. Read it at every quarterly review.
  • Small promises first. Stop saying "I'll try" and "let's catch up soon". Say yes with a date, or say no.
  • One courage rep a week. The honest feedback, the unpopular position, the admission. Log it.
  • The power rule. As you gain money, rank and strength, the test becomes how you treat people who can do nothing for you. That is the difference between Wayne and the men he fights.
  • Stay teachable. Ask one person a year, "What is the thing about me that nobody tells me?" Listen without defending.
