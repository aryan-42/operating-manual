03Scorecard

Score each domain from 1 to 10. Save a snapshot every quarter. The lowest score chooses your next focus.

Calibration: 3 = neglected. 5 = Floor standards mostly met. 7 = Strong tier met. 9 = Elite tier met and held for a year. Score from your ticked standards and your Baseline tests, not from how the week felt. Competence and Base were added in version 2, so snapshots saved before it show a dash for them. When either is your lowest score, raise it through its background routine first, and make it the focus only if it stays lowest for two quarters.
