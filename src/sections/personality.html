00Personality

Your traits decide which parts of this system will come easily and which will need scaffolding. Know them before you plan.

The one model worth using

The Big Five is the personality model used in research. Type systems with four letters or colours are fine for conversation and weak for prediction. Take a free public-domain Big Five inventory such as the IPIP-NEO, answer as you are and not as you wish to be, and record your results below.

What the evidence says

A review pooling 92 meta-analyses and more than 1.1 million people found conscientiousness to be the strongest non-cognitive predictor of performance at work. The effect is weaker in highly complex jobs, where judgement and ability carry more of the load.31 It is the trait this whole manual is designed to simulate from the outside: fixed cues, checklists, reviews and deadlines produce conscientious behaviour whether or not it comes naturally.
