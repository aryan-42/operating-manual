The Operating Manual

One system for body, mind, craft, money, presence, people and character. Built to be reopened every quarter for ten years.

00Start here

Read sections 01 to 04 once and fill them in. After that you only need the scorecard, one domain at a time, and the rhythm in section 13.

How to use this in the first week

  1. Today, 30 minutes. Score yourself in section 03 and save the snapshot. Do not think hard. First instinct is accurate enough.
  2. This week, 60 minutes. Fill in Direction (section 04). This decides what the next ten years are for.
  3. Pick one domain. Your lowest score is your constraint. Read that section and tick off its Floor standards before touching anything else.
  4. Set the rhythm. Put the weekly review in your calendar as a recurring 20-minute block.
  5. Come back every quarter. Rescore, save a snapshot, choose the next focus.

Two rules that protect the system

One focus per quarter. Every other domain runs at its Floor. Overhauling seven areas at once is the most reliable way to quit by week three.

The manual is finished. Do not rebuild it into an app, a dashboard or a prettier template until you have run it for 90 days. Building trackers feels like progress and is not training.

Everything you type or tick is saved automatically. Use the backup at the bottom of section 13 once a year.

01The standard

What "top 1% man" means here, stated so it can be measured.

You said you do not want the watered-down version. Agreed. Here is the version that is both ambitious and mathematically real.

The arithmetic of being rare

Being the best in the world at one thing is mostly unavailable. Being very good across every domain at once is rare and available. If four qualities were unrelated and you reached the top 30% in each, you would be in roughly the top 1% for the combination (0.3 × 0.3 × 0.3 × 0.3 = 0.8%). Top 25% in three is 1.6%.

Real traits are correlated, so the true figure is less extreme. The principle holds. Most people who are strong in one domain have let another collapse: the high earner who is unfit, the athlete who is broke, the achiever who is alone. The top 1% man is the one with no collapsed domain and one or two spikes.

The definition this manual uses

  • No domain below Strong. All seven domains at the Strong tier or better.
  • Two spikes at Elite. For you the natural spikes are Craft and one of Body or Presence.
  • Held for years. A standard you hold for one quarter is a stunt. The target is to still be there at 34.

The two characters, decoded

Both are written to look effortless. The traits underneath are trainable. Each also carries damage you should not copy.

02Principles

The psychology the system runs on, with an honest rating of how strong the evidence is.

Self-improvement content repeats findings that have not held up. Each principle below carries a rating: solid well replicated or consensus, useful real but smaller than advertised, contested do not build on it.

1. Confidence is a record, not a feeling solid

In Bandura's work on self-efficacy, the strongest source of belief in your ability is having actually done the hard thing before. Watching others and being encouraged help less.17 So you do not work on confidence directly. You stack small completed challenges and the belief follows. Every domain here starts with a Floor you can hit this month for that reason.

2. Pursue goals you endorse solid

Self-determination theory identifies three needs behind lasting motivation: autonomy (you chose it), competence (you are getting better), relatedness (you are connected to people while doing it). Motivation and wellbeing rise when they are met and fall when they are blocked.16 A goal you adopted to impress people fails the first test. Section 04 exists to filter those out.

3. Write if-then plans useful

"If it is 6:30 on Monday, Wednesday or Friday, then I go to the gym before anything else." A 2006 meta-analysis of 94 tests found a medium-to-large effect on goal attainment (d = 0.65).9 A much larger 2024 synthesis that corrected for publication bias put the effect far lower, between 0.15 and 0.35.10 Use it, because it costs nothing. Do not expect it to carry the load.

4. Imagine the obstacle, not just the win useful

WOOP: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan. Pairing a vivid goal with the specific inner obstacle and an if-then plan shows a small-to-medium effect across 21 studies (g = 0.34), and the authors note the true figure may be smaller.11 Pure positive visualisation is not part of this system.

5. Habits take months, and one miss is harmless solid

The study behind the "66 days" figure followed 96 volunteers for 12 weeks. The median time to automaticity was 66 days, with a range from 18 to 254, and missing a single day made no material difference.8 Only about half the participants fitted the habit curve at all. Plan on two to eight months per habit, and treat the first miss as nothing and the second in a row as an alarm.

6. Design the environment. Do not rely on willpower contested

The idea that willpower is a tank that drains has not survived testing. A preregistered project across 36 labs and 3,531 people found an effect near zero (d = 0.06), with the data four times more likely under no effect.12 Stop asking whether you have enough discipline today. Remove the decision: gym bag packed the night before, phone charging outside the bedroom, investments debited on salary day.

7. In knowledge work, hours are not the edge solid

Deliberate practice explains about a quarter of performance differences in games, a fifth in music and under 1% in professions.13 Practice still matters. In a career, the differentiators are which problems you work on, who gives you feedback and which opportunities you are near. Section 07 is built around that.

8. Learn by testing yourself, spaced out solid

A review of ten study techniques rated two as high utility: practice testing and distributed practice. Rereading and highlighting, the most common methods, rated low.14 For anything you want to keep: close the book, write what you remember, repeat after days and then weeks.

9. Skip the mindset and grit products contested

Growth-mindset programmes show an average effect on achievement close to zero (d = 0.05 across 63 studies), though a competing analysis finds real benefits for at-risk students.15 Grit turns out to be nearly the same thing as conscientiousness and only moderately related to performance.18 You do not need a special belief. You need the plain version of conscientiousness: do what you said, on schedule, checked.

10. Three thinking tools worth owning

  • The control split (Stoic). Sort every worry into "mine to act on" and "not mine". Act on the first list. Drop the second.
  • The thought record (CBT). Situation, automatic thought, evidence for, evidence against, balanced thought. Five lines on paper when your mind is running a story.
  • Values over moods (ACT). Ask "what would the person I am building do in the next ten minutes?" and do that with the mood still present.

05Body

Start here if you are unsure. It has the fastest feedback loop and it raises energy for everything else.

Floor month 1 to 3

Strong year 1 to 2

Elite year 3 to 6

The lifting and running numbers are common coaching benchmarks for a healthy man in his twenties, not research findings. Adjust for your frame and any injuries.

What the evidence says

  • Weekly dose. WHO guidance for adults is 150 to 300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75 to 150 vigorous, plus muscle strengthening for all major groups on 2 or more days.2
  • Cardio fitness is the big lever for lifespan. In 122,007 adults given treadmill tests, the fittest group had about a fifth of the death risk of the least fit, with no upper limit to the benefit. Low fitness carried risk comparable to or greater than smoking or diabetes. This was an observational study of clinic patients, so treat the size as indicative.3
  • Protein. Across 49 trials, extra protein improved strength and muscle gain from lifting, with no further benefit above roughly 1.6 g per kg per day.1
  • Training volume. Muscle growth rises with weekly sets per muscle. Ten or more beats fewer than five, and 12 to 20 hard sets a week is a reasonable range once you are trained.4
  • Sleep. The sleep-medicine consensus is 7 or more hours a night on a regular basis.5
  • Waist. NICE advises keeping your waist under half your height. A ratio of 0.4 to 0.49 indicates no increased risk.6
  • Creatine, optional. The best-studied supplement. 3 to 5 g a day maintains stores and is well tolerated at that dose.7 Check with a doctor first if you have any kidney concern.

Protocol

Months 0 to 6. Three full-body sessions a week. Each session: one squat pattern, one hinge, one push, one pull, one carry or core movement. Three sets of 6 to 12. When you hit the top of the range on all sets, add the smallest plate next time. Add two easy cardio sessions of 30 to 45 minutes at a pace where you can still talk.

Months 6 to 24. Four sessions, upper and lower split. Build to 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle per week. Keep two easy cardio sessions and add one interval session, for example four rounds of four minutes hard with three minutes easy.

Year 2 onward. Add a skill. Something with a coach, a ranking or an opponent. This is the Wayne part: capability, not only appearance.

Food. Hit protein first. Build meals around whole foods. Log everything for two weeks to calibrate your eye, then stop logging. Keep alcohol rare.

Safety. Pain that changes how you move means stop and see a physiotherapist. Learn the barbell lifts from a coach for the first month. It is the cheapest insurance you will buy.

Workbook · body

Weight, waist, height, resting heart rate, best lifts, 5 km time.
Update monthly. Date each line.
"If it is [day and time], then I [exact session] at [place]." Add one for the most likely obstacle.

How this domain fails

  • Changing programme every three weeks. Run one programme for at least twelve.
  • Training hard and sleeping six hours. Sleep is where the adaptation happens.
  • Chasing appearance with crash diets. The standard is performance and a stable waist, held all year.
  • Skipping cardio because lifting is more fun. The longevity data sits with cardio fitness.

06Mind

Attention, learning and emotional steadiness. This is where Specter's knowledge and Wayne's calm come from.

Floor

Strong

Elite

Protocol

Deep work block. Same time each day, phone in another room, one task written on paper before you start. Begin at 60 minutes and add 15 a month.

Reading that sticks. After each chapter, close the book and write what you remember. Review the note after a week and after a month. This applies the two techniques rated highest in the learning research: self-testing and spacing.14 Your commonplace book is the right home for these notes.

The Specter file. Pick the three subjects your career runs on and read the standard texts in each, not summaries. Depth is what lets you speak briefly and be right.

Pressure drill. Once a week do something that raises your pulse socially or mentally: ask a hard question in a meeting, cold-message someone senior, present without slides. Calm is trained by exposure.

Decision journal. Before any major decision, write the decision, what you expect to happen, and why. Read it six months later. It is the fastest way to find out how your judgement is actually performing.

Workbook · mind

The fix must change the environment. "Try harder" is not a fix.

How this domain fails

  • Consuming self-improvement content in place of doing the work. One book applied beats ten skimmed.
  • Treating low mood as a discipline problem. If you feel flat or anxious most days for two weeks or more, see a doctor or therapist. That is maintenance, the same as seeing a physio for a knee.

07Craft

Your work. At 24 this is the largest lever on income, options and self-respect.

Floor first 6 months in role

Strong year 1 to 3

Elite year 4 to 10

What the evidence says

Accumulated practice explains less than 1% of performance differences in professions, against 18 to 26% in sports, music and games.13 In knowledge work, grinding more hours on the same tasks is a weak strategy. What separates people is problem selection, feedback quality and proximity to opportunity. The protocol below is built on those three.

Your position

Engineering plus HR plus the ability to build AI automation is an uncommon combination. Most HR professionals cannot build and most builders do not understand how people decisions get made in a company. That intersection is your spike. Protect it by going deep, because breadth dilutes it.

The ladder

CredibilityYear 0 to 2
  • Be excellent at the job you were hired for. Nothing else counts until this is true.
  • Find the most painful manual process near you and automate it. Measure hours or money saved. That is your first number.
  • Choose managers over brand names. A strong boss in year one is worth more than a title.
AdjacencyYear 2 to 5
  • Move one step toward revenue: HR tech product, people analytics, consulting, total rewards or a business role. One step at a time is realistic. A direct leap rarely is.
  • Publish what you build. A portfolio does the selling when you are not in the room.
  • Keep a small HR and AI tool alive on the side with a fixed weekly time budget and written criteria for when to stop.
OwnershipYear 5 to 10
  • Take P&L responsibility, or found the company if the side tool has paying users.
  • Build a team. Your ceiling becomes the quality of people who choose to work with you.
  • Add income that does not depend on your salary.

The prep file

The whole Specter effect is one habit. Before any meeting that matters, write five lines: what they want, what I want, the three objections I will hear, my answer to each, and where I walk away. Ten minutes. Almost nobody does it.

Workbook · craft

Date, what I did, the number. This becomes every appraisal, CV and negotiation.

How this domain fails

  • Planning the third job while underperforming in the first.
  • Learning five tools shallowly. Depth in one visible thing beats a long skills list.
  • Letting the side project eat the main job. Credibility comes first.
  • Doing good work silently. If nobody with influence knows, it did not happen for career purposes.

08Money

Wayne inherited his. You build yours with a savings rate, time and rising income.

General information for an Indian salaried professional. This is not personalised financial advice. Tax rules and products change, so check current rules each April and use a SEBI-registered fee-only adviser for real decisions.

Floor first 12 months of salary

Strong year 2 to 4

Elite year 5 to 10

The "years of expenses" targets are arithmetic from a 40 to 50% savings rate held from age 24, before investment returns. They are checkpoints, not promises.

Order of operations

  1. Track one full month of spending before designing anything.
  2. Build the emergency fund in a liquid, boring place.
  3. Insure. Health cover now. Term life cover when someone depends on your income. Keep insurance and investment as separate products.
  4. Automate. A standing instruction on salary day into low-cost, broad index funds. Learn what EPF, NPS and PPF each do for you before choosing.
  5. Raise the rate with every raise. Lifestyle may grow, at half the speed of income.
  6. Then go after income. Saving has a ceiling. Earning does not. Section 07 is your real money plan.

What money does and does not do

A 2023 joint analysis by two research teams who had disagreed found that for most people happiness keeps rising with income. For the least happy fifth it stops rising at a point, so money does not fix misery that has other causes.20 The director of Harvard's 85-year study adds that beyond basic security, more wealth moves wellbeing only a little compared with relationships.19 Money is a strong tool for freedom and a weak one for meaning. Build it and do not confuse it with the mission.

Workbook · money

Assets minus debts. One line per quarter.
Example: wait 30 days on any purchase over a set amount. No loans for things that lose value.

How this domain fails

09Presence

How you look, sound and carry yourself. Competence people can see within thirty seconds.

Floor

Strong

Elite

Drills

  • Two-minute tape. Record yourself explaining one idea each week. Watch it once. Fix one thing: pace, filler words, eye line or posture.
  • Pause instead of filler. Silence reads as control. "Um" reads as searching.
  • Answer first. Conclusion in the first sentence, reasons after. Use it in email, meetings and interviews.
  • Three questions before one statement. In any new conversation, ask three real questions before offering an opinion. People remember being heard.
  • Speaking reps. Join a speaking club or volunteer for every presentation at work until the log reaches 25.
  • Negotiation prep. Their interests, your interests, your best alternative if this fails, your opening number and your walk-away. Written before the call.
  • Wardrobe. Fewer pieces, better fit. Two well-fitted suits or blazers, plain shirts, dark trousers, good leather shoes, one watch. Tailoring matters more than labels.

Workbook · presence

Date, audience, what went well, one fix.

How this domain fails

Copying the swagger without the preparation. On screen, the one-liners work because the character already won the case in the library the night before. Presence without substance gets found out quickly.

10People

The domain both of your models neglected, and the one the long-term research ranks first.

What the evidence says

  • Harvard's study of adult life has followed people since 1938. Its director's central finding is that the quality of close relationships shapes both happiness and physical health across a lifetime.19
  • Pooling 148 studies and 308,849 people, those with stronger social relationships had 50% greater odds of survival over the follow-up period, an effect comparable with well-established mortality risk factors.21

Floor

Strong

Elite

Protocol

  • The inner five. Name the five people who matter most. Each gets real time every week or fortnight, scheduled like training.
  • The move plan. Each time you change city, list who you are leaving and set a recurring call before you go. Friendships fade through logistics more than through conflict.
  • Mentors. Ask for one specific piece of advice, act on it, report back. That loop is how a senior person becomes invested in you.
  • Partner. Who you build a life with is likely the highest-leverage decision in this manual. Choose for character, shared direction and how you handle conflict together.
  • Repair fast. Apologise the same day when you are wrong. Say the appreciative thing out loud when you think it.

Protocol

14When it breaks

It will. The plan for failure is part of the system.

You missed a day

Nothing happened. In the habit research a single missed day had no material effect.8 Do the minimum viable day tomorrow. The rule is never two in a row.

You missed two weeks

  1. Do not restart on Monday. Restart within the hour, with the smallest version.
  2. Run only the three non-negotiables for seven days.
  3. In the weekly review, write what changed in your environment. Travel, a new job and a breakup are the usual causes. Fix the environment and leave your character out of it.

You have plateaued

  • Body: check sleep and food before changing the programme.
  • Craft: you have probably outgrown your feedback. Find a harder problem or a tougher reviewer.
  • Everything: a flat quarter after two strong ones is consolidation. Hold the Floor and wait.

You are burning out

Signs: sleep worsening, dreading things you used to like, getting ill often, snapping at people. Drop to Floor in every domain for two weeks, protect sleep, see your inner five. If it persists, see a doctor.

The standard has turned on you

"Best who can ever be" is a direction. Used as a verdict on your worth, it produces the Wayne failure: a capable, joyless man. Two checks each quarter. Did I enjoy anything that had no payoff? Would the people in my inner five say I have been good to be around? If either answer is no, that is the constraint to fix next, ahead of any score.

You compared yourself to someone

Compare with your own snapshot from a year ago. That is the only comparison with the same starting conditions.
